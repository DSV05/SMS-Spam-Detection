Methodology

How the system works

A single saved pipeline keeps training and inference preprocessing consistent.

01

Validate

Read the supplied SMS Spam Collection and check its labels and text fields.

02

Represent

TF-IDF converts message text into weighted word and phrase features.

03

Classify

Multinomial Naive Bayes estimates HAM and SPAM classes from learned features.

04

Serve

Flask exposes the fitted pipeline through this interface and a JSON endpoint.

Limitations & responsible use

  • The corpus is English-language and reflects its collection period.
  • Class probabilities are uncalibrated estimates, not certainty.
  • False positives and missed spam are possible; never rely on the label alone.
  • Do not submit sensitive messages or personal information.
